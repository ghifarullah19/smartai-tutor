# Product Steering

## Core Vision
PintarAI is an AI-powered virtual tutor designed to address the educational gap faced by high school students in rural ASEAN areas. It provides accessible, low-cost AI tutoring where physical teachers are scarce.

## Target Audience
- High school students in rural/remote areas.
- Users with potentially limited internet bandwidth or older devices.

## Key Features
1. **Interactive Chat Interface:** Intuitive conversational UI.
2. **AI-Powered Explanations:** Deep contextual answers powered by Google Gemini API.
3. **Session Management:** Unique user identification and history management.
4. **Optimized Experience:** Lightweight, responsive design with clear loading ("thinking") states.

## Future Product Enhancements
- Interactive practice questions.
- Speech-to-text integration.
- Curriculum-aligned learning paths.
- Multi-language support.
- Progress tracking dashboards.
